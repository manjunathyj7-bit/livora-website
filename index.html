Livora

Find a place you can trust.

Livora is a rental discovery app for India. Tell us where you need to live and what you can really afford, and we show nearby places with the real monthly cost, how fresh each listing is, and why it matches you.

See what is built How we show cost

Built to reduce uncertainty

Most listing sites make you guess. Livora is designed around the questions renters actually ask.

The real monthly cost

Rent plus maintenance plus estimated utilities, with deposit and move-in cost, so there are no surprises after you visit.

Freshness you can see

Every listing shows when the owner last confirmed it is available. Old ones are labelled, not passed off as current.

Honest verification

A listing is only called verified when a verification level has actually been set. Owners cannot give it to themselves.

Nearby first

Search by distance from where you want to live, with a budget range and the type of place you need.

Where the project is today

Livora is being built step by step. This list is kept honest: what works now, and what does not exist yet.

Working

  • Sign up and sign in, with a tenant or owner choice
  • Profile and sign out
  • Home with area, budget and category filters
  • Listing cards with real monthly cost and freshness
  • Listing detail page with cost breakdown and safety notice
  • Owner flow: add a listing as draft or published
  • Secure database with row-level security

Next

  • Use my current location when listing a property
  • Photo upload and gallery
  • Search with distance, and a map
  • Saved places and compare
  • Enquiries to owners and visit requests

Later

  • Student mode and commute time
  • Owner and listing verification
  • Reports and moderation tools
  • Chat and notifications
  • Payments and agreements, only after trust basics are solid
